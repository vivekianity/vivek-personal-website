05 / ABOUT ME

A researcher’s curiosity.
An educator’s purpose.

vb∕

I’m an Associate Professor at the Singapore Institute of Technology, working across cybersecurity and emerging computing technologies. I am also the Deputy CEO of Verbosecurity Pte. Ltd.

I completed my Ph.D. in Software Security at Nanyang Technological University and previously worked as a Research Scientist at A*STAR. My research spans software protection, mobile security, AI-enabled threat detection and quantum computing.

As Programme Leader for SIT’s Information Security degree from 2021 to 2024, I helped shape the education of the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.
